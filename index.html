Common questions

How long does a design take?

Posts and posters are usually ready in 1 to 2 days. Logos take 3 to 5 days.

How many changes can I ask for?

Every package includes free revisions. Extra changes cost a small fee, which I’ll confirm before I start.

What do I need to send you?

Your text, your logo if you have one, your brand colours and any designs you like.

How do I pay?

Message me and I’ll share the details. I accept JazzCash, Easypaisa and bank transfer.

Which files will I get?

PNG or JPG for posts, and print-ready PDF for posters.
